# Karachi Bites - Premium Redesign Direction

## Concept
"Spice Route" — Karachi ki food heritage ko modern UI mein dikhayein.
Colors saffron, cardamom green, and Karachi sunset oranges.

## Color Palette
- Primary: #D4A017 (Saffron Gold)
- Secondary: #1B4332 (Cardamom Green)  
- Accent: #C1121F (Chili Red)
- Dark: #1A1A2E (Midnight)
- Cream: #FDF8F0
- Surface: #2D2D44

## Typography
- Headlines: Bold, condensed, editorial feel
- Body: Clean, readable on cheap phones
- Urdu touch in accents

## Sections
1. Hero - Parallax food photography, layered
2. Categories - Horizontal scroll, tap-friendly
3. Menu Grid - Card-based with availability badges
4. How It Works - 3 steps (Order → Track → Enjoy)
5. Testimonials - Customer reviews
6. Footer - Contact, social, legal
